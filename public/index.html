Before your first call

The things people ask
before their first call

Is it actually free?

Yes. Calls cost nothing and there's no account or credit to buy. The tradeoff is that everyone else is using it too, so not every conversation will be a good one.

Do I need to sign up?

No. You can start a call without an account, and there's no profile for anyone to look up afterwards.

Can the other person see me?

No. Calls are voice only — there's no camera. You appear with a nickname, and contact details are hidden in chats with strangers.

What happens when I skip?

The call ends for both people straight away. Neither side keeps the conversation, and you're matched with someone new the moment you press start again.

What do you do about people who break the rules?

Reports are saved for review and the person is blocked from your queue. Messages that break the rules are removed automatically, and repeat offenders are paused.

Can I choose the country or the language?

Yes. Open Filters before you start to pick a language, interests, countries to match with — or countries to leave out.

Communication and guidelines

How to keep a conversation good

The same few rules apply to everyone on every call. They're short, and they're the reason strangers here are worth talking to.

Talking well

  • Say hello first. Most people are just as unsure as you are.
  • Ask open questions. The icebreaker card is there if you're stuck.
  • Want longer? Both of you press +3 min and the timer resets.
  • Not clicking? Press Next. Nobody needs a reason.

The rules

  • 18+ only. If someone seems younger, end the call and report it.
  • No hate, harassment, threats or sexual content.
  • Don't share phone numbers, emails, links or where you live. They're hidden automatically anyway.
  • Don't record or screenshot people without asking.

If something goes wrong

  • Next ends the call straight away for both of you.
  • Report sends the conversation for review.
  • Block means you'll never be matched with that person again.
  • Repeat offenders are paused automatically.
